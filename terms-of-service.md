## 1. The Spirit of the Agreement

Tool Library is a mutual-aid platform for neighbors to lend tools and exchange skills.
By creating an account you agree to participate in good faith: describe your listings
honestly, honor the loan commitments you accept, and treat other members with respect.
This is a community contract, not a commercial one — no fees are charged and no
purchases occur.

## 2. Accounts & Eligibility

- Accounts consist only of a `username` (login handle) and a public `alias`. The
  platform collects no email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal information.
- You are responsible for safeguarding your password and your recovery codes.
  **Recovery codes are the only account-recovery mechanism** — there is no email
  password reset, and lost credentials without recovery codes cannot be restored.
- One account per person. Do not share your credentials or allow others to act under
  your account.
- You must be able to enter into binding agreements under the law of your jurisdiction.
  Because the platform collects no personal data, it performs no age verification; the
  Operator may suspend accounts that appear to violate this requirement.
- The first account registered on an empty database automatically receives the
  `ADMIN` role. Administrative powers carry the accountability duties in the
  Code of Conduct.

## 3. Tool Safety & Responsibility

Tools are physical objects with real hazards. Borrowers agree to inspect a tool before
use, operate it only for its intended purpose, and follow all manufacturer safety
guidance. Lenders agree to disclose known defects and to keep listed tools in safe
working condition. The platform facilitates introductions; it does not inspect,
warrant, or certify any tool. Each member is responsible for their own safety
decisions.

## 4. Loan Obligations & Trust Consequences

When an owner approves your loan request, you are expected to return the tool on the
agreed date and in the condition received. Returning on time in good condition raises
your Trust Score by **+5**. Damaged or overdue returns lower it by **−15**. Trust
Scores are visible to other members and gate the community's willingness to lend to
you. Repeated abuse may result in account suspension by an administrator.

## 5. Acceptable Content & Listings

Listings, skills, messages, and profile content must be lawful and relevant to the
community's purpose. Prohibited:

- Weapons designed to harm people, illegal items, and counterfeit goods.
- Harassment, spam, doxxing attempts, and content that would violate the
  [Code of Conduct](code-of-conduct.md).
- Personal information — yours or anyone else's. The platform is designed to operate
  without it; do not put email addresses, phone numbers, or identifying details in
  listings, messages, or profiles.

Administrators may remove listings and suspend accounts that violate these rules.

## 6. Prohibited Uses of the Platform

You agree not to:

- Attempt to harvest, infer, or correlate members' personal information or identities.
- Circumvent, disable, or manipulate the approval gates that require human
  confirmation for destructive automated operations.
- Manipulate Trust Scores, the loan state machine, or another member's account.
- Scrape, overload, or deliberately degrade the Service, or interfere with its
  operation and backups.
- Use the Service to arrange anything unlawful in the Operator's jurisdiction.

## 7. Moderation & Enforcement

Enforcement follows the Code of Conduct: minor first offenses may receive a single
warning at moderator discretion; severe violations result in immediate account
suspension. Every moderation action is expected to be explainable to the affected
member, and decisions are reviewable and reversible through the community Matrix room.
Moderators are bound by the same code they enforce.
